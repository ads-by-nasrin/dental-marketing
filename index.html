How I approach real dental campaign problems

Each card is a real type of brief I work on: the problem clinics bring me, the approach I take, and what success looks like when it's working. Clinic details stay confidential - always.

Emergency Dentistry

“Our emergency ads eat budget but the phone barely rings”

The problem

Emergency intent mixed into general campaigns; ads running when the clinic can't answer; no call tracking.

My approach

Standalone emergency campaign, tight exact-match core, ad scheduling around real opening hours, click-to-call first, call tracking on every number.

What success looks like

Budget spent only in hours the clinic can treat - and every call counted, recorded as answered or missed.

Dental Implants

“Implant clicks are expensive and enquiries are tyre-kickers”

The problem

High CPCs wasted on research and price-shopping searches; all traffic sent to a generic page.

My approach

Separate implant structure by intent stage, a dedicated implant landing page, qualification built into the copy and the page - price context, consultation step, finance mention only where the clinic offers it.

What success looks like

Fewer, better enquiries: people who understand the treatment and the investment before they ever call.

Invisalign® / Aligners

“We're invisible for aligner searches in our own area”

The problem

Competitors and national brands dominate; the clinic's ads are buried in a general cosmetic campaign.

My approach

Own aligner campaign with location-layered ad groups, smile-assessment-led copy, and a landing page built around the consultation - not a services list.

What success looks like

Consistent consultation requests from the clinic's own catchment, at a cost per lead the clinic can plan around.

Tracking & Attribution

“Google says 40 conversions. Reception says 6 patients.”

The problem

Duplicate and soft conversions inflating reports; smart bidding optimising toward the wrong thing.

My approach

Full tracking rebuild: GTM + GA4 key events, primary vs secondary actions cleaned up, Enhanced Conversions, and offline import of booked/attended outcomes.

What success looks like

One number everyone trusts - and bidding that learns from patients, not button clicks.

Account Rescue

“We've spent for months with nothing to show for it”

The problem

Broad match sprawl, hundreds of irrelevant search terms, budget split across too many campaigns to learn anything.

My approach

Forensic audit first: search terms, negatives, structure, tracking. Then a paused, rebuilt structure you approve before a cent is spent.

What success looks like

Wasted spend cut at the source, budget concentrated where intent and margin actually are.

New Clinic Growth

“We're opening / expanding and need patients from month one”

The problem

No search history, no reviews flywheel yet, and a diary that needs filling with the right treatment mix.

My approach

Launch plan sequenced by intent: emergency and general first for volume, high-value treatments layered in as tracking proves out, budgets scaled on booked appointments.

What success looks like

A predictable enquiry pipeline the clinic can staff and plan around - not feast-or-famine marketing.

Questions

Before you ask

How fast will I see results?

Clicks and enquiries can start within days of launch, but fair judgement takes a full optimisation cycle - typically the first 4-8 weeks of data, search-term refinement and lead-quality feedback. High-value treatments like implants have longer decision cycles than emergency dentistry, and I'll set expectations per treatment, honestly.

Do I own my Google Ads account?

Yes - always. Campaigns are built in your account (or one created for you), you keep full access, and everything stays with you if we ever stop working together.

Who pays Google for the ads?

You do, directly. Your card, your account, your spend - I manage it, I never hold or mark up your media budget.

Why does tracking cost extra?

Proper tracking (GTM, GA4, call tracking, Enhanced Conversions) is separate technical work from monthly management - and it's the foundation everything else depends on. The Essential Setup is €500-600; advanced setups are quoted by scope.

Will you launch campaigns without telling me?

Never. Builds are prepared paused, you approve the structure and budget, and go-live happens only on your explicit approval.

Can you guarantee a number of patients?

No - and you should be wary of anyone who does. What I commit to is a sound structure, honest tracking, weekly optimisation, and reporting that shows you exactly what your spend produced.

Do you work with clinics in my area?

I work with dental clinics remotely and build every campaign around your local market - your catchment, your competitors, your treatment mix. Book a call and we'll talk through your area specifically.
